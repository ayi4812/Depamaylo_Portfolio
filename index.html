01 / About

A little about me

A student who enjoys solving problems

I am a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology student at FEU Alabang, specializing in Web and Mobile Applications. My coursework and projects have taught me to plan features, design user-focused interfaces, work with databases, and test and debug until things work as intended.

My biggest project is Baylo, our capstone, a web and mobile community bartering platform where I served as Project Manager and Web Lead Programmer. I have also worked on an e-commerce proposal, app wireframes and mockups, and earned certifications.

I value clear communication, attention to detail, and continuous learning. I hope to contribute to meaningful projects, learn from experienced professionals, and keep improving as a developer.
